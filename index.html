How it's done

Prep is the reason it lasts

Most floors that fail were rolled straight onto slick concrete. Here is what a Quamcrete floor gets instead, and why the free estimate is worth booking.

Diamond grind the slab

We open the concrete with grinders, not an acid wash, so the coating actually bonds.

Fill every crack and pit

Chips, cracks and pitted spots get patched flat before any color goes down.

Broadcast the flake

Color chips are thrown to full refusal, then scraped back for an even, full floor.

Seal with polyaspartic

A UV stable top coat that shrugs off hot tires, oil, salt and sun for years.

Back in the space in a day

Walk on it that evening, park on it that weekend. No week of tape and fumes.
